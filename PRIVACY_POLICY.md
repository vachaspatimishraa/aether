# Privacy Policy for AetherCalc Pro

**Last Updated:** November 8, 2026

AetherCalc Pro ("the Application") is an offline-first, precision scientific, graphing, programmer, and analytical calculator developed by AetherLab ("we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled within the Application.

---

### 1. Zero Data Collection Policy
AetherCalc Pro operates entirely offline on your device. We believe mathematical calculations and personal analytics should remain strictly confidential.
- **No Personal Information Collected:** We do not collect, store, transmit, or share names, email addresses, phone numbers, location data, or device identifiers.
- **No Analytics or Telemetry:** The Application does not contain any third-party analytics SDKs, tracking pixels, or diagnostic trackers.
- **No Advertising:** AetherCalc Pro is 100% ad-free. No advertising networks or tracking IDs are included.

---

### 2. Device Permissions
AetherCalc Pro requests only the minimal permissions required to provide tactile user interface interactions:
- `android.permission.VIBRATE`: Used exclusively to provide haptic feedback when pressing calculator buttons. No data is gathered or recorded through this permission.
- The Application does **not** request internet access, contacts, camera, microphone, storage, or location permissions.

---

### 3. Local Data Storage
- All calculation history, memory register values, unit preferences, and custom matrices are stored strictly locally in your device's private sandbox storage.
- You can clear your calculation history at any time directly within the app using the "Clear History" button.
- Uninstalling the Application completely removes all locally stored data.

---

### 4. Children's Privacy
Because AetherCalc Pro does not collect any personal data whatsoever, it is fully compliant with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) and the Google Play Families Policy. It is safe for students of all ages.
